About Bella

Your host in Da Nang

Bella has worked in Da Nang real estate for 10 years, renting and selling apartments with a focus on foreign clients. She manages owners' apartments in Sam Tower, The Ponte, Panoma 1 and Panoma 2.

Her story

Ten years in Da Nang real estate

I'm Bella. For 10 years I've advised clients on Da Nang apartments and brokered apartment sales and rentals. Much of my work is with clients from abroad, so I show apartments in person, or on a video call if you haven't arrived yet.

Over those years I built my own system for managing apartments and grew it to cover four buildings: Sam Tower, The Ponte, Panoma 1 and Panoma 2. If you rent an apartment there, you message me for anything you need.

I also work for property owners. I lease and run their apartments, aiming for the best return and a property that holds its value for years.

I work in one market: Da Nang apartments, both high-end homes and rentals. I'll show you options that fit your budget and plans, and I'll explain the terms before you sign.
